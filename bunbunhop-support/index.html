BunBun Hop Support

Need help with BunBun Hop on iOS? Use this page for support, account questions, purchases, ads, and gameplay issues.

Email Support

Common Help

  • For purchase issues, use Restore Purchases in the app when available and include your Apple receipt details if you email.
  • For rewarded extra-life ads, make sure your connection is stable and try again after a short wait if an ad is unavailable.
  • For account, leaderboard, or sign-in issues, include your in-game name and the sign-in method you used.
  • For gameplay bugs, include the level, boss, outfit, and what happened right before the issue.
