Sky Stack Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 2026

Overview

Sky Stack is designed for entertainment. We respect your privacy and aim to collect as little information as possible. We do not sell your personal information.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information only as needed to operate the game:

Account Information

  • Email address, if you sign in
  • Username or display name
  • Authentication identifiers from Apple or Google sign-in services

Gameplay Information

  • High scores
  • Leaderboard rankings
  • Basic game statistics
  • Purchase status for premium features such as ad removal

Technical Information

Third-party services may automatically process limited technical data such as:

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Crash diagnostics
  • Anonymous usage analytics
  • Advertising identifiers, if ads are enabled

Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

We do not intentionally collect:

  • Precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages
  • Browsing history
  • Sensitive personal information

How We Use Information

We use collected information only to:

  • Allow sign-in and account access
  • Save scores and leaderboard rankings
  • Restore purchases
  • Improve performance and stability
  • Prevent cheating, abuse, or fraud
  • Serve advertisements, if applicable

Third-Party Services

Sky Stack may use third-party providers whose own privacy policies apply, including:

  • Apple
  • Google
  • Firebase
  • Google AdMob

These services may process limited information necessary for authentication, analytics, ads, crash reporting, or purchases.

Advertising

If advertisements are shown in the game, ad providers may use limited device information to deliver and measure ads in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Users who purchase ad removal may no longer see certain advertisements.

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as reasonably necessary to operate the game, maintain leaderboards, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

Sky Stack is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

Depending on your device and region, you may be able to:

  • Delete the app
  • Disable ad tracking in device settings
  • Use Sign in with Apple privacy options
  • Request deletion of account-related data, where applicable

California Privacy Notice

California users may have rights under applicable law regarding access, deletion, and certain disclosures related to personal information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the game after updates means you accept the revised policy.
